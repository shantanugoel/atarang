# Practice Tools Phase 2 Manual Tests

Use headphones for recording tests. Run the matrix with at least one two-stem
song and one four- or six-stem song.

## Pitch and recording

- Play vocals, bass, guitar (when available), and the full mix at −12, −7, +7,
  and +12 semitones at 50%, 75%, and 100% speed.
- Confirm speed never changes when pitch changes and pitch never changes when
  speed changes.
- Listen for dropouts, clipping, metallic artifacts, and loss of stem sync.
- Record a short take at a non-zero pitch and non-100% speed. Compare the live
  backing with the saved backing and exported M4A.

## Manual metronome

- Enter a known constant BPM and align a downbeat at the song playhead.
- Verify quarter, eighth, triplet, and sixteenth subdivisions.
- Verify the accent, level, and metronome-only controls.
- Let a constant-tempo song play for at least five minutes and listen for click
  drift. Repeat across an A–B boundary.
- Change speed and confirm the click remains aligned to the same musical beats.

## Repetition and ramp

- Set a three-pass target and confirm the completed/remaining display and stop
  at the third B boundary.
- Add a two-second pause and confirm neither backing nor click sounds during it.
- Ramp from 50% to 100% by 5% every two repetitions.
- Hold the ramp, resume it, and use Stop Structured Practice during playback
  and during an inter-repetition pause.

## Saved sections

- Save, rename, switch among, and delete at least four sections.
- Close and reopen the song and confirm all sections remain.
- Confirm selecting a section enables its loop and seeks to A.

## One-pass loop recording

- With a loop and count-in enabled, press Record from before, inside, and after
  the loop.
- Confirm the playhead moves to A before the count-in, the count-in is absent
  from the take, and recording stops at B without starting a second pass.
- During recording, confirm loop, speed, pitch, target, and workspace controls
  are locked.
- Alternate Reference and Latest Take immediately after recording.
- Confirm microphone/backing level editing and export still work.
